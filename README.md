This project is a simulated U.S. international tax compliance engagement prepared in Excel for a fictional client, Daniel Wong, a U.S. citizen residing and working in Hong KongThis is an educational and simulated portfolio project. The client, financial information, foreign tax amounts, and supporting documents are fictional or project assumptions.
The workbook is not intended to constitute an actual U.S. tax return, tax advice, or filing determination. Certain tax positions and filing requirements are intentionally identified as "Review Required" where a professional tax engagement would require additional facts, current IRS guidance, or detailed technical analysis.
The project is designed to demonstrate an ability to organize international tax information into structured workpapers, trace source data through calculations, identify compliance considerations, document review points, and present findings in a professional Excel-based format.
The exchange rate of USD 1 = HKD 7.80 and the simulated Hong Kong income tax amount are project assumptions used consistently for illustrative purposes.
